\section{Research Map Beyond The Article's Theorem Spine}
\label{sec:appendix-b-research-map}

This non-load-bearing map records adjacent investigations and the separate
almost-prime program. None of the properties below is used as a premise of
a theorem in this article. The proof status is quoted from each property's
own record: where a proof is conditional, the condition family named there
is repeated here, and the record itself states the full hypotheses. In the
status column, \textbf{Proved} means mathematically proved in the record,
and Stainless verification is not claimed for any row.

Table 1 lists the properties treated only in their own property records,
that is, the properties left out of this article; for those rows only the
investigation line and the proof status vary. Table 2 lists the eighteen
properties with a direct connection to this article and states that
connection per row.
