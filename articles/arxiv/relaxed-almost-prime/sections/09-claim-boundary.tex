\section{Claim Boundary}

This article proves:

\begin{enumerate}
\item positivity of the relaxed weight implies a prime plus an integer with at
   most two prime factors;
\item the exact one-divisor local factor and periodic boundary remainder;
\item the exact shifted-divisor reduction to prime-progressions;
\item the exact inverse-residue and nonprincipal-character bilinear family; and
\item failure of scalar-density Type-II orthogonality on the complete wheel.
\end{enumerate}

It does not prove:

\begin{itemize}
\item positivity for any unbounded family of heads;
\item the accumulated prime-progression estimate;
\item a locally adapted Type-II estimate;
\item a new proof of Chen's theorem; or
\item any twin-prime statement.
\end{itemize}
