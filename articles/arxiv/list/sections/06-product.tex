\section{Product Properties}
\label{sec:product}

The product operation, from singleton identity through concatenation
distributivity to positivity.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \href{https://github.com/thiagomata/prime-numbers/blob/list-article-v1.0.0/src/main/scala/v1/chapter3/list/properties/ListProduct.scala}{Singleton product}:
    $\operatorname{product}(x :: \text{empty}) = x$
  \item \href{https://github.com/thiagomata/prime-numbers/blob/list-article-v1.0.0/src/main/scala/v1/chapter3/list/properties/ListProduct.scala}{Product pull-out element}:
    $\operatorname{product}(x :: L) = x \cdot \operatorname{product}(L)$
  \item \href{https://github.com/thiagomata/prime-numbers/blob/list-article-v1.0.0/src/main/scala/v1/chapter3/list/properties/ListProduct.scala}{Product over concatenation}:
    $\operatorname{product}(A \mathbin{\texttt{++}} B) = \operatorname{product}(A) \cdot \operatorname{product}(B)$
  \item \href{https://github.com/thiagomata/prime-numbers/blob/list-article-v1.0.0/src/main/scala/v1/chapter3/list/properties/ListProduct.scala}{Commutativity of product}:
    $\operatorname{product}(A \mathbin{\texttt{++}} B) = \operatorname{product}(B \mathbin{\texttt{++}} A)$
  \item \href{https://github.com/thiagomata/prime-numbers/blob/list-article-v1.0.0/src/main/scala/v1/chapter3/list/properties/ListProduct.scala}{Positive product}:
    product of all-positive list is positive
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Singleton Product}

The product of a singleton list containing $x$ is $x$.

\begin{gather*}
\forall\, x \in \mathbb{S} \\
\operatorname{product}(x :: L_e) = x
\end{gather*}

\textbf{Proof:}

\begin{equation*}
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{product}(x :: L_e) &= \operatorname{head}(x :: L_e) \cdot \operatorname{product}(\operatorname{tail}(x :: L_e)) && \text{[by definition of product]} \\
&= x \cdot \operatorname{product}([]) && \text{[by definition of head and tail]} \\
&= x \cdot 1 && \text{[product of empty list is 1]} \\
&= x \quad \blacksquare && \text{[Q.E.D.]} \\
\end{aligned}
\end{equation*}

\subsection{Product Pull-Out Element}

A single element can be factored out of the product of a concatenated
list.

\begin{gather*}
\forall\, listA, listB \in \mathbb{L}, \forall\, e \in \mathbb{S} \\
\operatorname{product}(listA \mathbin{\texttt{++}} (e :: listB)) = e \cdot \operatorname{product}(listA \mathbin{\texttt{++}} listB)
\end{gather*}

\textbf{Proof by induction on $listA$}\par\nopagebreak

\textbf{Base case}: $listA = L_e$\par\nopagebreak

\begin{equation*}
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{product}(L_e \mathbin{\texttt{++}} (e :: listB)) &= \operatorname{product}(e :: listB) && \text{[by definition of append and cons]} \\
&= e \cdot \operatorname{product}(listB) && \text{[by definition of product]} \\
&= e \cdot \operatorname{product}(L_e \mathbin{\texttt{++}} listB) \quad \blacksquare && \text{[Q.E.D.]} \\
\end{aligned}
\end{equation*}

\textbf{Inductive step}: Assume for $listA$, prove for
$\operatorname{head}(A) :: listA$.

The full Scala verification code is in Appendix~A.12.

\subsection{Product over Concatenation}

Product distributes over list concatenation.

\begin{gather*}
\forall\, listA, listB \in \mathbb{L} \\
\operatorname{product}(listA \mathbin{\texttt{++}} listB) = \operatorname{product}(listA) \cdot \operatorname{product}(listB)
\end{gather*}

\textbf{Proof by induction on $listA$}\par\nopagebreak

\textbf{Base case}: $listA = L_e$\par\nopagebreak

\begin{equation*}
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{product}(L_e \mathbin{\texttt{++}} listB) &= \operatorname{product}(listB) && \text{[by definition of append]} \\
&= 1 \cdot \operatorname{product}(listB) && \text{[1 is multiplicative identity]} \\
&= \operatorname{product}(L_e) \cdot \operatorname{product}(listB) \quad \blacksquare && \text{[Q.E.D.]} \\
\end{aligned}
\end{equation*}

\textbf{Inductive step}: For $\operatorname{head}(A) :: listA$, the
recursive definition of product and the inductive hypothesis give the
result.

This property is verified in

The full Scala verification code is in Appendix~A.13.

\subsection{Commutativity of Product}

Product is invariant under swapping concatenated blocks.

\begin{gather*}
\forall\, listA, listB \in \mathbb{L} \\
\operatorname{product}(listA \mathbin{\texttt{++}} listB) = \operatorname{product}(listB \mathbin{\texttt{++}} listA)
\end{gather*}

\textbf{Proof:}

\begin{equation*}
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{product}(listA \mathbin{\texttt{++}} listB) &= \operatorname{product}(listA) \cdot \operatorname{product}(listB) && \text{[Product over Concatenation]} \\
\operatorname{product}(listB \mathbin{\texttt{++}} listA) &= \operatorname{product}(listB) \cdot \operatorname{product}(listA) && \text{[Product over Concatenation]} \\
&= \operatorname{product}(listA) \cdot \operatorname{product}(listB) && \text{[Commutativity of multiplication]} \\
&= \operatorname{product}(listA \mathbin{\texttt{++}} listB) \quad \blacksquare && \text{[Q.E.D.]} \\
\end{aligned}
\end{equation*}

This property is verified in
\href{https://github.com/thiagomata/prime-numbers/blob/list-article-v1.0.0/src/main/scala/v1/chapter3/list/properties/ListProduct.scala}{\texttt{ListProduct::\allowbreak productConcatCommutative}}.
The full Scala verification code is in Appendix~A.14.

\subsection{Positive Product}

If every element of a list is strictly positive, then the product is
strictly positive.

\begin{gather*}
\forall\, elements \in \mathbb{L} \\
(\forall\, x \in elements,\ x > 0) \implies \operatorname{product}(elements) > 0
\end{gather*}

\textbf{Proof by induction on $elements$}\par\nopagebreak

\textbf{Base case}: $elements = L_e$\par\nopagebreak

\begin{equation*}
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{product}(L_e) &= 1 > 0 \quad \blacksquare
\end{aligned}
\end{equation*}

\textbf{Inductive step}: For $\operatorname{head}(e) :: tail$, we have
$e > 0$ and $\operatorname{product}(tail) > 0$ by inductive hypothesis.
The product of two positive numbers is positive.

The full Scala verification code is in Appendix~A.15.
\par}
