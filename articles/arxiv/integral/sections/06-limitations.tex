\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

This article builds upon the foundational assumptions and constraints
established in the earlier work
\href{https://github.com/thiagomata/prime-numbers/blob/master/articles/chapter3/list.md}{Using
Formal Verification to Prove Properties of Lists Recursively Defined}~%
\cite{mata2026lists}.

Specifically:

\begin{itemize}
  \item The focus remains on lists of unbounded integers
    (\texttt{BigInt}), without support for generalized numeric types via
    abstraction or type classes.
  \item Recursive functions such as $\operatorname{sum}$,
    $\operatorname{head}$, $\operatorname{tail}$, and concatenation are
    reused from the prior work \cite{mata2026lists} and are not redefined
    here.
  \item Due to the recursive nature of these definitions, stack overflows
    may occur with extensive lists, but correctness and verifiability take
    priority over performance.
\end{itemize}
