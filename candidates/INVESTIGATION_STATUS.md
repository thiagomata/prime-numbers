# Candidate Investigation Closure Matrix

**Date:** 2026-08-03

## Purpose

This matrix is the stable handoff for the candidate investigation. It answers
a different question from whether a candidate is proved:

> Has each materially important route been examined deeply enough that a new
> investigator knows the established reduction, the failed shortcuts, and the
> genuinely new ingredient required to continue?

The population, scope, quantifier, and proof-status vocabulary follows the
[Research Vocabulary](../VOCABULARY.md). Candidate hypotheses remain open
unless their own notes say otherwise.

## Closure Labels

- **Outcome/terminal formulation:** useful as a conclusion, not an independent
  mechanism.
- **Problem boundary established:** the important reduction and failed generic
  routes are recorded; continuing needs the named new arithmetic ingredient.
- **Supporting open route:** mathematically meaningful, but currently weaker,
  stronger than necessary, or dependent on the main frontier.
- **Intentionally deferred:** investigated enough to show why it is not a
  present priority and what prerequisite would justify reopening it.
- **Externally known; method-specific proof open:** the target objects exist
  classically, but deriving positivity from this project's relaxed weights is
  a separately scoped future proof program.

## What Is Still Important

The matrix leaves three explicit mathematical fronts, not an open-ended list
of candidate ideas:

1. **Twin-prime frontier:** prove signed mean-square or cross-layer
   cancellation for #23 sufficient for #24.
2. **Alternative local mechanism:** revisit #4 only after proposing a useful
   quantitative cyclic-run bound that survives the exact run-three
   counterexample and composes with a local-block theorem.
3. **Almost-prime fallback:** execute #25's already-scoped Type-I/Type-II
   program if the research goal expands from twin-prime structure to a
   method-specific Chen-type result.

The Danger-Annulus Decomposition property and the refined #2/#16 statements provide a quantified reopen
path within the deferred local-mechanism family. The effective annular
destruction allowance is smaller, but so is the unknown pre-filter population:
no positive lower bound for `L_D` and no favorable exact annular expansion with
proved `B_D,U_D` is known. This is not a fourth established front or a solved
reopening, and it does not displace #23 -> #24 as the primary twin-prime
continuation.

The first is the continuation of the current main line. The third is the only
high-value direction that is genuinely different in analytic strength. The
second remains falsifiable but presently contains two unproved premises and is
not ranked above the signed-energy route.

## Maintenance Rule

Update this matrix only when one of the following happens:

- a candidate's main quantifier changes;
- a new proved property changes its dependency or priority;
- an exact auxiliary statement is refuted;
- a named reopen trigger is satisfied; or
- a genuinely distinct mechanism is added.

Finite reinforcement alone should update the candidate's evidence section,
not its closure classification.
