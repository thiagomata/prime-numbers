## Experiment and Assumptions

Starting heads: 5,7,11,13,17,19,23,29,31. Destination rules: next prime;
first prime >=2p; first prime with q^2>p^3; first prime >p^2. Always q>p.
Endpoint window [q,q^2), both pair endpoints inside. All old filters <p;
batch p<=r<q. Compute h(a), the number of distinct batch primes hitting either
endpoint, and I_k=sum binomial(h(a),k), I_0=C. Test lower bounds L_1,L_3,L_5
and the full alternating sum. Calculate raw unique removed-value count T.

No complete old cycle is materialized for large moduli: its local values are
generated by exact gcd predicates, and the complete-period G_p by its proved
product formula. This is not a claim that a huge cycle was enumerated.

## What is Learned

- Odd inclusion-exclusion truncations are lower bounds, not exact survivor
  counts unless higher intersections vanish. This is classical algebra.
- If p^d>=q^2, each endpoint has at most d-1 distinct batch prime factors;
  the pair has at most 2(d-1), also at most the batch size.
- Nonrecursive descriptions alone are not progress; this experiment tests
  whether a bounded-order certificate adds practical information.
- For p=31, q=967: C=31005, T=73853, L_1=-9598, L_3=5591,
  L_5=7649, exact survivors=7688. Raw T and pair incidence I_1 differ.
- The cubic remainder is exactly sum over h>=4 of binomial(h-1,3)N_h.
  Pollack, Theorem 6.8 (printed p.177), documents the classical Bonferroni
  inequalities; this is not a newly discovered sieve mechanism.
- Each moment can be evaluated using old pair residues and 2^k CRT hit classes
  per k-subset of batch filters, counted in copy-index intervals by floor sums.
- Odd truncations improved monotonically in the sample, but this is not a
  general theorem: their consecutive differences can have either sign.
- Durable result: `properties/sieve-sequence/research/batched-survival-overlap-audit-2026-10-27.md`.
  Updated research scope: `candidates/batched-window-survival.md` and its index.

## Alternatives and Risks

Recording only positive final counts would repeat a primality computation; the
question is certification by a specified low-order bound. Multiplicity moments
still require local arithmetic and do not establish a uniform theorem. Finite
positive results may fail at larger heads. Runtime cost is not a mathematical
objection. Maximum endpoint below about one million keeps this audit bounded.

## Failed Paths

The L_1>0 certificate fails in all nine tested square jumps (negative L_1),
although actual survivors are positive. Thus the uncorrected pair-hit sum cannot
certify these cases. Revisit only with an overlap correction, a smaller batch,
or a proved sharper destruction budget. This does not refute survival.

## Open Concerns

No generic threshold or positive asymptotic local bound has been established.
The moment diagnostic tests complete hit patterns; it does not supply independent
uniform moment estimates. Naive accumulation of CRT floor errors may overwhelm
the certified margin. Attribution is classical Bonferroni/CRT, not novelty.
