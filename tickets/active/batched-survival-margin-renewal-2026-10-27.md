## What is Learned

The late-band corollary is unconditional on twin existence: actual bounds
and fixed positive/finite constants establish an eta once and for all.
Under a hypothetical last twin, at least half of the guaranteed family
must be rejected earlier than q^eta. The band is narrow in logarithmic
exponent, not additive length. This is first-rejection location control,
not proof that those candidates reach the later cutoff.

The switched forms v and uv-2 admit an actual uniform prime-pair upper
bound. The singular series is uniformly bounded, and the source's uniform
L4 estimate implies the count by a small-arc argument. Summing by the
unique smaller factor controls first rejections without double counting;
the full factor range still has a nonvanishing reciprocal-prime mass.

Actual old-candidate abundance is now established at the original square
rule through the stronger rough Chen input, not through an improvement to
the DHR lower function. Deleting at most sqrt(X+2) square partners leaves
at least a positive constant times H/log squared p candidates in the upper
half of Wq. One endpoint is prime; the other is prime or has exactly two
distinct factors greater than Z. Here log Z/log p tends to12/11 and
log Z/log q to6/11. The same argument works for fixed alpha>11/6.
The remaining issue is semiprime partners, not raw square-rule supply.

and B=absolute N+Rcap+J=o(F). Thus Lnext>=L-B. This preserves a positive
next certificate from a fixed positive old-F margin eventually, but does not
